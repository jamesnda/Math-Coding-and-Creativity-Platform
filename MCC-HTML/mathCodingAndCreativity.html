05 — Two distinct journeys

MCC opens the door.
You choose what comes next.

Math Coding & Creativity (MCC) is a youth-focused initiative that helps students enjoy mathematics, discover its everyday applications, and explore technology and possible career directions.

M-TECH School is separate. It is a school for those who choose to pursue deeper, sustained learning in technology. MCC is not a technical school, and joining M-TECH is not a requirement for taking part in MCC.
